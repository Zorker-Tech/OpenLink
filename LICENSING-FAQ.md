# Licensing FAQ

This FAQ summarizes the [Zorker Openlink Source-Available License](./LICENSE)
for convenience. The [LICENSE](./LICENSE) controls in case of conflict.

## Is Zorker Openlink open source?

No. It is source-available. The source is published, but the License restricts
distribution, operational use, and commercial use above a revenue threshold.
These restrictions are not compatible with the Open Source Definition. Do not
describe the Software as open source.

## Can I use it for personal projects?

Yes. Individuals may use, build, run, and modify the Software for private,
non-commercial purposes. Personal use is still subject to the License: you may
not redistribute the Software or modified versions, and you may not use it to
provide services to third parties.

## Can I use it at work?

Yes, if your organization's Annual Net Revenue, together with its affiliates on
a consolidated basis, is USD 3,000,000 or less. The use must be internal to your
organization. You may not make the Software available to customers, clients,
partners, or other third parties, and you may not include it in a product or
service you deliver to them.

## What happens if we grow above USD 3,000,000?

You must notify Haokir within 30 days after the end of the fiscal year in which
the threshold is exceeded, and obtain a Commercial License within 60 days
after the end of that fiscal year. Until then, your existing internal use may
continue during that period. After that period, Commercial Use requires a
Commercial License. See [COMMERCIAL.md](./COMMERCIAL.md).

## How is Annual Net Revenue calculated?

It is the consolidated net revenue of your organization and its affiliates for
the most recently completed fiscal year, determined under GAAP or IFRS, less
returns, allowances, discounts, rebates, and sales/use/VAT and similar taxes.
Intercompany revenue is excluded. Non-USD amounts are converted at the average
exchange rate for the fiscal year. If you do not prepare consolidated financial
statements, use combined revenue with intercompany revenue eliminated.

## Can I modify the source?

Yes, for personal use or internal use. Modifications and derivative works remain
subject to the License. You may not distribute modified versions or make them
available to third parties without a Commercial License.

## Can I host it for my customers?

No, not without a Commercial License. Hosting, operating, or making the Software
available to third parties is Operational Use, regardless of your revenue.

## Can I include it in a product I sell?

No, not without a Commercial License. That is Distribution. A Commercial License
is required even if your revenue is at or below the threshold.

## Can I contribute a fix or a feature?

No. Haokir does not accept external contributions. Do not open pull requests or
send patches. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md).
