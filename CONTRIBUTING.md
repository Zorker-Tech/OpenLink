# Contributing

Haokir Inc. does not accept external contributions to Zorker Openlink.

The source code is published so that you can inspect, build, and use it under
the terms of the [LICENSE](./LICENSE). Publication is not an invitation to
contribute.

- Do not open pull requests, send patches, or submit code, documentation, or
  assets.
- Unsolicited contributions will be closed without review.
- If Haokir invites a contribution in writing, Haokir will require a signed
  contributor license agreement (CLA) before accepting it.
- Do not submit confidential information, trade secrets, or third-party code
  that you do not have the right to contribute.

Separately Licensed Components are governed by their own contribution policies.
This policy applies to the Zorker Openlink Software, not to those components.

## Bug reports and security reports

Use the official contact at https://www.hydite.com. Do not include sensitive
information in public reports. Security reports should be sent privately to
Haokir Inc. through the official contact channel.
